# Asset wishlist (free, safe to use in a game you sell)

Download these when you have a minute and leave them in Downloads. All are free; licenses noted.

## Combat: weapons, animations, enemies
- **Universal Animation Library** (Quaternius, CC0): 120+ humanoid animations (sword swings, rolls, hits, deaths) that retarget onto our character rig. Biggest upgrade for "better fighting animations".
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
- **Universal Animation Library 2** (Quaternius, CC0): more combat and movement animations.
  https://quaternius.com (search "Universal Animation Library 2")
- **Bestiary - Dungeon Monsters Kit** (Quaternius, CC0): rigged, animated monsters (slimes, skeletons, goblins and more) for enemy variety.
  https://quaternius.com/packs/bestiary.html (or the itch.io page)
- **Ultimate Monsters** (Quaternius, CC0): cute animated monsters, good for silly enemies around the world.
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html
- **Cute Animated Monsters Pack** (Quaternius, CC0): already approved earlier, not downloaded yet.
  https://quaternius.com/packs/cutemonsters.html
- **Fantasy Props MegaKit** (already downloaded) has weapons: swords, axes, staffs, shields.
- Already owned in your Asset Store account: **Free Low Poly Fantasy RPG Weapons** (Pure Poly), **STYLIZED Fantasy Armory** (Daniel Mistage), **FREE Low Poly RPG Weapons Demo** (Ventuar3D). These are free; import them through Unity's Package Manager (My Assets).

## Buildings and decor (to match your Pinterest vibe)
- **Ultimate House Interior Pack** and **Ultimate Furniture Pack** (Quaternius, CC0): cozy interiors for shops and homes.
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatehouseinterior.html
- **KayKit - Medieval Hexagon Pack** and **KayKit Furniture Bits** (Kay Lousberg, CC0): cute stylized pieces.
  https://kaylousberg.itch.io
- Already owned: **[FREE] Low Poly Sci-Fi Computers Demo** (retro computer props for the overgrown-tech buildings) and **Ultimate Pc Computer Pack**.

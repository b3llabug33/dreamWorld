# Orion's greeting (first time you arrive in the town square)

Orion (her rainbow stag beetle) walks up the first time you reach the town square. Structure is from
your notes; the lines are a DRAFT for you to rewrite in your own words.

## Opening
Orion skitters up to you. If you back away or don't answer right away, she says:
**Orion:** "wait wait, don't be scared! i'm a beetle, but i'm a person too. i promise i don't bite. (much.)"

**Orion:** "hello traveler! what brings you here?"

Choices (pick any, come back to the menu after each; "that's all" to leave):

### fight
*explain:* "out past the paths, the woods get wild. shroomlings, moss golems, worse. roll to dodge,
click to swing, and lock on with Q. inside the town and on the paths you're safe. nothing can hurt you
there."

### barter
*explain:* "some folks here trade item for item instead of coins. bring them what they're looking for
and they'll swap you something good."

### sell
*explain:* "shops will buy what you find out there. monster drops, extra materials, things you made.
open your bag at a shop and pick something to sell it."

### collect
*explain:* "chop trees for wood, break rocks for stone, mine ore from glowing veins, and gather thread
from cotton puffs and old spiderwebs. you can turn all of it into decor for your house."

### i don't know
*go over all of it:* Orion explains fight, barter, sell and collect one after another, then:
"it's a lot, i know. you'll get the hang of it."

## Then (after the menu)
**Orion:** "oh! and if you want a place of your own, you can buy a plot of land at the land office.
that's where you'll build your base." *(more info: plots cost 100 coins, your home computer keeps your
coins and items safe, and you wake up there if you get knocked out)*

**Orion:** "you'll meet a lot of characters around here. vampires, elves, a very loud mayor. talk to
everyone. some of them need help, and helping people is how you'll find quests."

**Orion:** "come find me anytime. i'll be around the fountain."

## Notes
- After the first time, talking to Orion again reopens the same menu (a tutorial you can revisit).
- "collect" should point at the nearest tree/rock with a little marker the first time.
